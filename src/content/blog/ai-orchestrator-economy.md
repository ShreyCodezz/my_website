## the shift from teams to orchestrators

I’ve been thinking about this for a while. In like 4 years, companies probably won’t hire the way they do right now. Not big teams, not a bunch of people doing small parts of a project. Instead, it’s gonna be one person managing a bunch of AIs. And honestly, we can already see it starting.

## compression is already happening

Right now, if you know how to use AI properly, you can do things that used to take 3–4 people. Coding, designing, writing, editing, it’s all getting compressed into one person with the right tools. So imagine this but faster, better, and just normal.

## from doing the work to directing it

I don’t think jobs will disappear, but they’ll change a lot. You won’t be hired just to do work anymore, you’ll be hired to control how work gets done. Instead of doing everything yourself, you’ll tell one AI to generate ideas, another to write, another to refine, and then you connect everything. You’re not the worker at that point, you’re the one directing it.

Basically an AI orchestrator.

## why companies will prefer this

And if you think about it, companies would obviously prefer this. Why hire 5 people when 1 person with AI can do the same thing faster and cheaper? So yeah, I do think companies will start hiring fewer people, but the people they do hire will need a completely different skillset.

Not just hard work, more like thinking clearly, giving the right instructions, knowing which tools to use, and spotting mistakes.

## the real competition

The part most people are missing is this: it’s not gonna be humans vs AI. It’s gonna be Human + AI vs Human + AI. That’s where the real competition is.

## the question that actually matters

So I don’t think the question in the future will be “will AI take my job?” It’ll be “do I know how to use AI better than the next person?” Because the ones who do won’t need a team anymore, they’ll already have one.
